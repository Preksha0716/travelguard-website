Built around the emergency moment

Features from the TravelGuard prototype concept.

🆘

One-tap SOS

Start an emergency flow without navigating complex menus.

🌐

Multilingual

English and Hindi input can be structured and translated into responder-friendly output such as Malayalam.

🧾

Emergency Context Card

Medical type, severity, people affected, location, language, battery and network in one compact view.

🫧

Temporary Emergency Bubble

A consent-controlled incident workspace with live context, acknowledgement, status and expiry.

📴

Offline-first

Queue a signed emergency packet locally and retry when connectivity returns. Delivery is not claimed until acknowledged.

🔐

Privacy by design

Location sharing begins only when Emergency Mode is activated. No continuous tracking is required by the concept.
